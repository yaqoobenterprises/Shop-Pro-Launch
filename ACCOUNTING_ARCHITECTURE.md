# Accounting architecture

**Proposed book of record.** Each shop has one base-currency general ledger. A posted journal contains two or more lines whose debits equal its credits in exact minor units. Smart entry, manual vouchers, trade documents, stock and corrections call one posting service. Drafts, pending instructions and suggested matches have zero posted effect. The accountant and owner must approve the policies in the [decision register](IMPLEMENTATION_ROADMAP.md) before activating a shop.

## Proposed starter Chart of Accounts

Codes are suggested display and ordering values. Each account has an immutable shop-scoped ID, class, normal side, parent, reporting role and postability. Headings are non-postable. Unused optional leaves can remain inactive. Create a separate leaf for each actual bank, wallet and cash drawer. Pin each control role to its approved account ID. Name changes preserve references, historical labels and audit history.

| Class / range | Posting leaves (normal side in parentheses) | Controls and reporting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Current assets 1000–1499 | 1010 Cash (Dr), 1020 Bank (Dr), 1030 Wallet (Dr), 1100 Trade receivables (Dr), 1150 Vendor advances (Dr), 1200 Goods inventory (Dr), 1250 Recoverable input tax (Dr), 1300 Prepayments (Dr), 1350 Other receivables (Dr) | Cash leaves link to a payment account. Receivables require customer and open-item detail. Inventory requires stock valuation detail. Tax leaves require enabled policy. |
| Non-current assets 1500–1999 | 1500 Equipment at cost (Dr), 1590 Accumulated depreciation (Cr) | Display carrying value net of its contra asset. Record manually approved depreciation separately. |
| Liabilities 2000–2999 | 2010 Trade payables (Cr), 2050 Customer advances (Cr), 2100 Output tax payable (Cr), 2150 Accrued expenses (Cr), 2200 Short-term loan principal (Cr), 2500 Long-term borrowings (Cr) | Payables require vendor and open item. Advances retain party identity until applied. Separate interest from principal. |
| Equity 3000–3999 | 3010 Owner capital (Cr), 3020 Owner drawings (Dr), 3100 Retained earnings (Cr), 3990 Opening balance clearing (either side, target zero) | Keep contributions, drawings and retained earnings distinct. Clearing is restricted to migration review and must clear before sign-off. |
| Revenue 4000–4999 | 4010 Goods sales (Cr), 4020 Service revenue (Cr), 4090 Sales returns and allowances (Dr), 4500 Other operating income (Cr) | Separate gross sales, contra-revenue and applicable tax. Each posting has one mapped statement row. |
| Cost of sales 5000–5999 | 5010 Cost of goods sold (Dr), 5100 Direct service materials (Dr) | Inventory valuation issues post to cost of goods sold. A stock purchase initially increases inventory under the proposed perpetual model. |
| Operating expenses 6000–6999 | 6010 Rent (Dr), 6020 Utilities (Dr), 6030 Wages (Dr), 6040 Supplies (Dr), 6050 Bank fees (Dr), 6060 Depreciation (Dr), 6070 Stock loss (Dr), 6090 Other operating expenses (Dr) | Require a reason and supporting reference for stock differences or exceptional amounts. |
| Other results and tax 7000–8999 | 7010 Other income/gains (Cr), 7100 Finance expense (Dr), 7200 Other losses (Dr), 8010 Income tax expense (Dr) | Enable mappings with accountant approval. No assumed tax rates, liability thresholds or statutory filing outputs. |

Use a reason-specific, approved counteraccount for each adjustment. A drawer shortage, an owner's contribution and an omitted receipt can all affect cash and require different counterparts. An unresolved legacy amount stays in a review schedule. Routine entry cannot use opening clearing to force balance. Support additional leaves inside existing classes with validated parents, unique shop codes, history-preserving archive and effective-dated statement mappings.

## Records, keys and relationships

| Proposed record | Minimum fields | Integrity boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shop book, account, period | shop, immutable base currency and exponent after posting, timezone, policy version, fiscal calendar, account ID/code/class/normal side/status, period start/end and state | Unique shop code, no cycles, one posting period per date. Period states open, closed and reopened with actor, reason and time. |
| Journal draft / revision | UUID, shop, version, origin (smart/manual/document/import), intent ID, date, type, narration, reference, source revision, lines, creator/editor | Versioned optimistic edits. Both modes can open and modify one draft, including party and allocation details. |
| Journal entry | ID, shop, human voucher number/series, business date, posted instant, document kind/ID, intent ID, confirmed revision, currency, narration, reference, preparer/poster/approver as applicable, policy version, reversal link and reason | Unique (shop, series, fiscal scope, number), unique (shop, intent ID), and unique source posting per document revision and event. Committed entries are immutable. |
| Journal line | ID, shop, journal ID, ordered line number, ledger account ID, debit minor, credit minor, memo, optional party/open item/source line/stock movement and cash-flow classification | Unique line order. Exactly one positive side, other side zero. Same shop for every reference, exact base currency, active posting leaf, required dimensions on control accounts. |
| Document, open item, settlement allocation | Shop, kind, document/line IDs, party, issue/effective/due dates, tax snapshot, original amount, linked journal, amount applied, settlement date and reversal | Balances derive from dated postings and allocation events. Lock open items during settlement. Prevent over-allocation and cross-party/shop/currency application. Separate advances from outstanding invoices. |
| Stock movement and valuation detail | Shop/item/location, quantity in fixed unit precision, value in base minor units, source line, issue-cost provenance, business date, posting link | Every valued event ties to stock-control lines. Pure location moves net to zero entity quantity and value. Protect concurrent issues. |
| Statement line and match | Shop, bank account, statement ID, external line/ref, date, signed amount, match group, matched book lines, status, reviewer | Uniqueness by source identity within statement/account. Permit many-to-one/one-to-many with explicit equal group totals. Preserve original lines and match/unmatch events. |
| Posted receipt, policy and audit event | Intent ID, normalized payload hash, actor, saved receipt, version, timestamp, source IDs and event type | Receipt lookup authorizes the caller. Append-only change history includes approvals, reversals, annotations, open/close, exports and match overrides. |

Use composite shop keys for foreign references even when entity IDs are globally unique. Validate exact integers on the wire as decimal strings. Use bounded integers or exact numerics for storage and widened exact aggregates. Quantities, unit prices and tax rates may need higher precision than posted money. Calculate extensions at agreed precision, round once at the approved boundary and allocate any remainder deterministically to identified lines. Preserve the inputs, rule version and rounded values. Reject out-of-range input before posting. Never balance an unrepresentable input by inserting an unexplained cent.

## Command path and lifecycle

| Operation | Typed input / result | Server behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `previewJournal` | Draft revision + shop selection → normalized lines, totals, source changes, warnings, blocking errors, policy version and expiring preview reference | Resolve canonical IDs, business date, currency, period, open items, enabled roles and posting rights. Display missing fields or the exact imbalance. |
| `postJournal` | Intent key, expected draft/source revision and confirmed preview → posted receipt, validation error, conflict or access denied | Reauthenticate actor and membership. Bind confirmation to shop, action, normalized payload and version. Revalidate state after acquiring locks. An expired/changed preview returns for review. |
| `lookupPosting` | Authorized shop and stable intent key → committed receipt or unresolved status | Supports timeout, reconnect and refresh. Keep the original key across RPC, outbox, tabs and import retry. Two distinct same-value purchases retain distinct keys. |
| `reverseJournal` / `replaceJournal` | Original ID and version, reason, effective date, reviewer and stable new intent → linked receipt(s) | Invert every line and dimension exactly. For a replacement, atomically link reversal plus corrected posting. Prevent duplicate full reversals. Require document credit/settlement reversal where linked open items or stock would otherwise diverge. |
| `annotateJournal` | Entry ID, expected metadata revision, signed note with author/time | Append a note. Preserve posted narration and financial lines. UI can show current annotation and original document together. |

Within one database transaction, claim the intent, compare the normalized input for repeated keys, lock relevant draft, source, period and controlled balances in stable order, allocate the voucher number, validate every line and the **whole journal**, then save header, lines, allocations, valued movements, audit and receipt together. Reject an imbalance of one minor unit. Issue numbers only on final posting using the approved sequence scope. Preserve cancelled/voided numbers and record any gaps according to policy. Reject conflicting reuse of a key even if the amount matches. A concurrent equivalent request sees the same receipt after commit. Give ordinary clients access to commands and scoped reads, with no direct mutation of posted headers, lines or balances.

Persist draft → reviewed revision → posted receipt. Drafts may be withdrawn. After posting, show “reversed by” and “replaces” from linked entries. A V2 equivalent of restore is an explicitly confirmed reinstatement with a fresh linked journal. Statements sum the original journal and all effective reversal/replacement journals through the reporting date. Excluding the original as well as counting its reversal would subtract twice. Closing and posting must serialize on the same period. Display effective business date and actual posting time separately on all late entries.

## Posting templates

All examples are prospective and use a single base currency. For control accounts, both entry modes collect the supporting party/open-item or stock detail before posting.

| Reviewed event | Debit | Credit | Evidence / distinction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cash service sale 100.50 | Cash 100.50 | Service revenue 100.50 | Cashbook income, receipt or service invoice paid at sale. Link the payment so revenue is recognized once. |
| Pay utilities 32.25 | Utilities 32.25 | Cash 32.25 | Payment for a previously booked bill instead debits Payables and applies the bill. |
| Owner contributes 500 / draws 12 | Cash 500 / Owner drawings 12 | Owner capital 500 / Cash 12 | Separate vouchers, both outside the operating result. |
| Transfer 100 from drawer to bank | Bank 100 | Cash 100 | One voucher, two cash-account lines, zero total cash change. Record a bank fee as a separate expense line when confirmed. |
| Borrow 1,000 / repay 110 including 10 interest | Bank 1,000 / Loan 100 and finance expense 10 | Loan 1,000 / Bank 110 | Preserve lender/source and settlement date. |
| Invoice for 100 plus illustrative tax of 10 | Trade receivables 110, tagged to customer/invoice | Revenue 100 and output tax 10 | A later receipt of 40 debits Bank and credits Receivables 40. Unpaid amount is 70. No second revenue posting. |
| Bill for supplies 100 plus approved recoverable tax 10 | Supplies 100 and input tax 10 | Trade payables 110, tagged to vendor/bill | A vendor payment debits Payables and credits Bank. Configure nonrecoverable tax treatment before enabling taxes. |
| Credit/refund, advance | Customer credit debits returns/allowances and applicable output tax, customer advance debits cash | Credit note credits Receivables or refund liability as applicable, advance credits Customer advances | Link credit to original tax and quantities. Paying a refund reduces the amount owed to the customer. Vendor credits mirror the original expense/asset and recoverable tax as applicable. |
| Stock purchase and issue | On receipt/bill recognition debit Inventory at approved cost. On sale debit COGS at recorded issue cost. | Payables or Bank on purchase, Inventory on issue | Link the sales revenue journal and valuation event. Restock only quantities actually returned and accepted. Credit at original traceable cost where policy permits. |

### Worked checks for developers

**Legacy boundary.** Opening Cash 200, Bank 0. After +100.50 income, −32.25 expense, +500 contribution, −12 drawing, +0.55 and −0.30 adjustments, a transfer of 100 gives Cash **656.50**, Bank **100.00**, combined **756.50** and legacy operating result **68.25**. The differences have reasons, directions and no legacy profit effect. An approved V2 adjustment counterpart and its impact must appear in the mapping schedule. A two-sided journal alone cannot determine that counterpart. Historical void/restore and amount edits retain their observed cashbook results. See the baseline evidence in the [product blueprint](PRODUCT_BLUEPRINT.md).

**Stock and credit fixture.** In a fresh synthetic shop with no tax or opening balances, receive three items at 60 on vendor credit. Sell two on customer credit at 100 each. Collect 80 from the customer and pay 60 to the vendor from Bank. Remaining stock is one item valued at 60, Bank 20, Receivables 120, Payables 120, revenue 200 and COGS 120. Assets 200 equal liabilities 120 plus earnings 80. Unclosed trial balance has debits of 320 and credits of 320. A repeated settlement key must preserve each figure.

## Financial statements and reconciliation

| Reader | Calculation and linkage | Publication checks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General journal / GL | Journal ordered by business date, posted sequence and stable ID. Account detail starts with brought-forward debit-minus-credit, adds period lines, then displays closing on its debit/credit side. Credit-normal screens can show credit-positive with explicit Dr/Cr. | Opening + movement = closing. Include archived leaves and all effective reversals. Link every row to voucher and source. |
| Trial balance | Compute per-account net as of date from posted lines. Put positive net on debit side and negative net magnitude on credit side. Show brought-forward, period debits/credits and closing separately. | Total period debits = total period credits and total closing debit balances = total closing credit balances. A balanced trial balance alone cannot prove classification. |
| Income statement | For the period, net sales after approved returns, less cost of sales = gross profit, then operating costs and separately mapped other results, finance and tax. Show basis, comparison and mappings. | Tie each row to its account movements. Exclude owner flows and inter-account transfers. Keep legacy income less expenses separately labeled. |
| Balance sheet / equity roll-forward | At date, report classified assets, liabilities and owner equity, including accumulated unclosed earnings through that date. Opening equity + contributions − drawings + earnings and approved other equity movements = closing equity. | Assets = liabilities + equity. Choose explicit year-end closing or virtual earnings roll-forward. With posted closing journals, include only unclosed earnings once and exclude close transfers from operating comparisons. |
| Cash flow | Direct presentation by operating, investing and financing classes. Classify counterpart cash components of split entries, eliminate internal transfers, reconcile opening cash + classified flows = closing cash. | Classify every external cash component. Flag unmapped lines and bases crossing cutover. Show excluded noncash activity separately. Owner approves the cash-equivalent set and mappings. [IAS 7 overview](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-7-statement-of-cash-flows/) |
| Receivables / payables | Open item at date = original amount less dated allocations/credits through that date. Age open balances by approved due-date buckets, show unapplied advances separately. | Sum party balances to their respective control GL balances at the same as-of date. Require party, source and open-item details on every control posting including opening items. |
| Inventory | Reconcile opening quantity/value + receipts − issues ± returns/counts to closing by SKU/location and inventory GL. Valuation and revenue recognition policy require approval before stock launches. | Test returns at prior cost, fractional quantities, backdating and final-unit rounding. FIFO and weighted average are established alternatives for interchangeable items. [IAS 2 overview](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-2-inventories/) |
| Bank / drawer | Capture statement balances or a signed physical cash count. Compare statement closing + deposits in transit − outstanding payments ± explained statement errors to book closing adjusted for approved book-side differences. | Resolve book-side differences with explicit journal previews (e.g., fees). A match changes reconciliation state only. Record cutoff, outstanding list, evidence and reviewer. Reopen with reason if subsequent entries affect a signed reconciliation. |

Use inclusive shop calendar dates and a consistent server snapshot for all readers. Daily and monthly groups use the same definition as their drill-down. Emit zero for a verified complete no-activity interval, carry as-of balances forward, and identify unavailable/opening-incomplete periods. For a range spanning the transition, show legacy and journal sections with dates and coverage. Publish a combined figure only where basis and mappings have been reconciled and approved.

## Historical cashbook and offline compatibility

Prefer a prospective cutover. First capture the shop boundary, effective definitions, report snapshot, current balances, dated movements, archives and unresolved device operations. Verify backups on an isolated restore. Reconcile each payment account and all independently supported noncash opening balances. Include open invoice/bill schedules and opening stock quantities/values when applicable. If unsupported, disclose the missing opening and hold the affected statement or module. Preserve shop IDs, source IDs, original values, aliases, old receipts and historical export formats.

Seed a single signed opening voucher at the approved boundary, with an itemized source-to-line manifest and independent reviewer. Each opening component is unique per shop, boundary and source. Legacy `account_posted=false` rows can already be reflected in seeded cash balances. Never replay them over the opening. Review legacy `investment`, unlinked adjustments, retail-only expenses and unattributed device records individually. Maintain the old cashbook as dated read-only history for converted shops. A historical reconstruction needs its own authorized mapping, reconciliation and reversible rehearsal.

Keep one financial writer per shop and epoch. Fence older writers at the service/database boundary, including already-open old tabs, direct legacy routes and delayed imports. Reconcile outstanding intents before activation. Late queues use original keys to look up an existing receipt. Route unmapped or changed drafts to a signed-in review inbox. Offline users can prepare either voucher type against a dated reference cache. Queue a confirmed intent only under the approved sync policy, bind to actor/shop/book epoch, persist after IndexedDB transaction completion and distinguish pending from posted. Recheck rates, stock, open items, rights and period on reconnection. Changed impact always requires a new preview. Never silently substitute another shop, date or account.

**Release evidence.** Retain per-shop before/after amounts, counts, source links and all explained differences. Require zero unexplained balance difference, zero duplicate logical postings, matched opening schedules, passage of tenant and period tests, and a rehearsed restore. The rollout and stop conditions are specified in the [roadmap](IMPLEMENTATION_ROADMAP.md).
